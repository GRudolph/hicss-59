## Timing Strategy

- **Title slide:** 10-15 seconds (just introduce yourself)
- **Concept slides:** 30-45 seconds each
- **Image/diagram slides:** 20-30 seconds (let audience read)
- **Case study slides:** 45-60 seconds each (more detail)
- **Key transition slides:** 15-20 seconds
- **Thank you:** 10 seconds

**Total estimated time: 12-14 minutes** (leaves buffer for questions or slight overruns)
